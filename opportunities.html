Student & Postdoc Opportunities

I am always on the lookout for highly determined and self-motivated students and postdocs who want to make a real impact in Applied AI for Healthcare. Research topics are intentionally diverse — from core technical contributions, to clinical application studies, to human-computer interaction research.

Important — Please Read Before Reaching Out

If you are genuinely interested in joining my group, please fill out the application form below. Please do not send a generic email with your CV — it will not receive a response.

Open Research Tracks

💻
Core Technical Contributions
Novel AI algorithms, architectures, and methodologies — multimodal learning, foundation models, LLMs, agentic systems, interpretable AI.
🏥️
Clinical Application Studies
For students with a clinical or biomedical background wanting to explore AI in their specific domain of expertise.
🧑‍🤙
Human-Computer Interaction
Co-design, usability research, and end-user perspective studies on how patients and clinicians relate to AI systems.
🌟
Unconventional Backgrounds Welcome
Non-traditional pathways are actively valued. Reach out with your unique perspective.
Women and individuals from all gender identities and underrepresented backgrounds are strongly encouraged to reach out. Diversity of thought and lived experience makes research stronger — and computing as a field has significant progress still to make on this front. I am committed to building an inclusive, equitable, and supportive research environment.
