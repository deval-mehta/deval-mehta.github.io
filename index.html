Deval Mehta

Senior Lecturer in Digital Health

School of Computing Technologies, RMIT University · Melbourne, Australia

I am a Senior Lecturer in Digital Health at the School of Computing Technologies at RMIT University, and an Adjunct Senior Research Fellow at the Department of DSAI, Faculty of IT at Monash University.

I am an interdisciplinary research leader with academic, clinical, and industry experience in developing Translational AI systems for healthcare. My expertise spans multimodal learning, foundation AI models, LLMs, clinician-in-the-loop design, and patient-centered studies for diverse healthcare problems.

Before joining RMIT in July 2026, I was a Research Fellow at the Department of Data Science & AI at Monash University from 2021–2026, where I also co-founded the Augmented Intelligence and Multimodal Analytics (AIM) for Health Lab at the Faculty of IT in 2023.

have also had 2.5 years of industry research experience post my PhD at IBM Research Labs in Australia and Panasonic R&D Center in Singapore. I completed my PhD from the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University in 2019.
