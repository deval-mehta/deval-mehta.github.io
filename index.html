Deval Mehta, PhD
RMIT University

I am a Senior Lecturer in Digital Health at the School of Computing Technologies at RMIT University. I am an interdisciplinary research leader with academic, clinical, and industry experience in developing Translational AI systems for healthcare. My expertise spans multimodal learning, foundation AI models, LLMs, and clinician-in-the-loop design for diverse healthcare problems.

Before joining RMIT in July 2026, I was a Research Fellow at the Department of Data Science & AI at Monash University from 2021–2026, where I also co-founded the Augmented Intelligence and Multimodal Analytics (AIM) for Health Lab at the Faculty of IT in 2023.

I have also had 2.5 years of industry research experience post my PhD at IBM Research Labs in Australia and Panasonic R&D Center in Singapore. I completed my PhD from the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University in 2019.

Research Interests &
Collaborative Opportunities

The design of futuristic healthcare AI technologies requires a highly multi-disciplinary framework and end-users should be at the core of it.

I focus on developing AI systems that are actually translatable in clinical practice, and for this I believe a huge collaboration is required between different disciplines — including clinicians, health economists, social scientists, human-computer interaction researchers, ethical scientists, sustainability and environment researchers, and most importantly patients and end-users themselves.

Thus, I collaborate with a range of discipline scientists from different institutions, hospital organizations, and patient community organizations, and have developed the following themes of research:

01
Technical Advancements
My research has focused primarily on multimodal learning with explainability and interpretability as a major component. More recently, with the advent of LLMs and Agentic AI systems, it has focused on mitigating hallucinations and grounding the knowledge of these systems in clinical evidence and guidelines. Student & Postdoc Opportunities

📢
Important Notice Before Reaching Out

If you are genuinely interested in joining my group, please fill out the application form below. Please do not send a generic email with your CV — it will not be responded to.

Fill out the Application Form

I am always on the lookout for highly determined and self-motivated students and postdocs who want to make a real impact in Applied AI for Healthcare. The research topics are intentionally diverse and could range from core technical contributions, to clinical application studies (students from clinical backgrounds who want to explore AI in their domain), to Human-Computer Interaction studies where co-design and end-user perspectives are the primary aim.

Additionally, if you come from an unconventional background but are eager to explore this field, I am also happy to consider your application — and may in fact view it positively.

Open Research Tracks

💻
Core Technical Contributions
Developing novel AI algorithms, architectures, and methodologies — multimodal learning, foundation models, LLMs, agentic systems, and interpretable AI.
🏥️
Clinical Application Studies
For students with a clinical or biomedical background who want to explore how AI can be applied to their specific domain of expertise alongside technical collaborators.
🧑‍🤙
Human-Computer Interaction
Co-design, usability research, and studies of end-user perspectives on healthcare AI. Understanding how patients, clinicians, and carers interact with and relate to AI systems.
🌟
Unconventional Backgrounds Welcome
Coming from a background that doesn’t fit neatly into the above? I actively value diverse and non-traditional pathways — please reach out with your unique perspective.
⚔️
Women and individuals from all gender identities and underrepresented backgrounds are strongly encouraged to reach out. Diversity of thought, lived experience, and perspective makes research stronger — and computing as a field has significant progress still to make on this front. I am committed to building an inclusive, equitable, and supportive research environment.

Supervision Philosophy

I am a highly flexible supervisor who understands that students and postdocs work in diverse ways and need different kinds of support at different times. My goal is to ensure that after your time with me, I can genuinely help you achieve your goals — whether that is in industry or academia, drawing on my prior experience in both worlds.
