Patient & Clinician-Centred Design

Co-designing AI with patients, clinicians, and social scientists to ensure real-world acceptability and adoption.

I have initiated several collaborations with social scientists and patient/community organisations to co-design AI systems that are genuinely acceptable and adaptable in practice. Understanding end-users’ perspectives on these technologies is fundamental.

Co-design, Adoption & Ethics of Healthcare AI
Working with the Epilepsy Foundation, HCI researchers (Monash), and community organisations to build AI that reflects real patient and clinician needs. Papers in preparation — please contact me directly for details.
