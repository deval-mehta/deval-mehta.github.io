Research Interests & Collaborative Opportunities

Translational AI for healthcare, developed across technical, clinical, and patient-centred dimensions.

"The design of futuristic healthcare AI technologies requires a highly multi-disciplinary framework and end-users should be at the core of it."

I focus on developing AI systems that are actually translatable in clinical practice. This requires collaboration across disciplines — clinicians, health economists, social scientists, HCI researchers, ethical scientists, and most importantly patients and end-users themselves.

I collaborate with researchers from diverse institutions, hospital organisations, and patient community organisations across three interconnected themes:

01

Technical Advancements

My research focuses on multimodal learning with explainability and interpretability as a central concern. More recently, I focus on mitigating hallucinations in LLMs and grounding their knowledge in clinical evidence and guidelines.

Human Activity Monitoring & Action Recognition
Automated motion analysis and action recognition for marker-less clinical assessment of neurological and movement disorders.
  • "Towards automated and marker-less Parkinson disease assessment: predicting UPDRS scores" — D. Mehta* et al. — CVPR 2021
  • "DeepActsNet: A deep ensemble framework for action recognition" — Asif U., D. Mehta et al. — Pattern Recognition 2023
  • "Privacy-Preserving Early Detection of Epileptic Seizures in Videos" — D. Mehta* et al. — MICCAI 2023 ⭐ Top 15%
Vision-Language Models
Combining visual and language understanding for interpretable medical image analysis, concept-guided diagnosis, and clinical report generation.
  • "WISE: Weak-Supervision-Guided Step-by-Step Explanations for Multimodal LLMs" — Yiwen J., D. Mehta* et al. — EMNLP 2025
  • "Enhancing Interpretable Image Classification Through LLM Agents and Conditional CBMs" — Yiwen J., D. Mehta* et al. — ACL 2025
  • "Interpretable Few-Shot Retinal Disease Diagnosis with Concept-Guided Prompting of VLMs" — D. Mehta* et al. — IPMI 2025
Foundation Models & Agentic Systems
Large-scale medical foundation models and AI agents grounded in clinical evidence and guidelines for diverse healthcare tasks.
  • "Knowledge Tree Driven Contextualized Instruction Tuning for Epilepsy Drug Recommendation" — Pham K., D. Mehta* et al. — MICCAI 2025
  • "M3CoL: Harnessing Shared Relations via Multimodal Mixup Contrastive Learning" — Kumar R., D. Mehta et al. — TMLR 2025
  • "Adaptive Transformer Modelling of Density Function for Nonparametric Survival Analysis" — Zhang X., D. Mehta et al. — Machine Learning (Springer) 2025
Interpretable Concept-Based Learning
AI models where predictions are grounded in human-understandable visual and clinical concepts, enabling transparency and accountability for clinical adoption.
  • "Enhancing Interpretable Image Classification Through LLM Agents and Conditional CBMs" — Yiwen J., D. Mehta* et al. — ACL 2025
  • "Interpretable Few-Shot Retinal Disease Diagnosis with Concept-Guided Prompting" — D. Mehta* et al. — IPMI 2025
  • "Out-of-distribution detection for long-tailed and fine-grained skin lesion images" — D. Mehta* et al. — MICCAI 2022 ⭐ Top 15%
02

Healthcare Domains

The translational nature of my AI research is grounded through close partnerships with clinical domain experts. I have built four active pillars and am actively expanding.

AI for Neurology
Epilepsy management, stroke prediction, Parkinson’s assessment, and multiple sclerosis analysis. Developed with clinical partners at Monash, Alfred Health, and internationally.
  • "Knowledge Tree Driven Instruction Tuning for Epilepsy Drug Recommendation" — MICCAI 2025
  • "Multi-modal CT Perfusion-based Deep Learning for Predicting Stroke Lesion Outcomes" — AJNR 2025
  • "Privacy-Preserving Early Detection of Epileptic Seizures in Videos" — MICCAI 2023 ⭐ Top 15%
  • "Towards automated and marker-less Parkinson disease assessment" — CVPR 2021
AI for Dermatology
Trustworthy and clinically deployed AI for skin lesion analysis. Work implemented in GP clinics across Australia and New Zealand via Molemap Pty. Ltd.
  • "Large Multi-task Vision AI Model for Dermatology" — D. Mehta* et al. — JEADV 2025 (10% accept rate)
  • "Assessment of Image Quality on the Diagnostic Performance of DL Models" — JEADV 2024
  • "Out-of-distribution detection for long-tailed and fine-grained skin lesion images" — MICCAI 2022 ⭐ Top 15%
AI for Histopathology Analysis
AI systems for automated analysis of whole slide images to support oncology and tissue pathology workflows.
  • "TPMIL: Trainable Prototype Enhanced Multiple Instance Learning for WSI Classification" — Yang L., D. Mehta et al. — MIDL 2024
AI for Diverse Hospital Settings
AI solutions for ICU clinical handover, infection prevention and control, emergency triage, and augmented clinical workflows.
  • Papers in progress — please contact me for current work.
AI for Cardiology Expanding — Seeking Partnerships
I am actively looking for clinical collaborators in cardiology to develop and translate AI tools for cardiac disease management.
AI for Human Kinetics & Wearables Expanding — Seeking Partnerships
Interested in partnerships exploring wearable sensor data and human movement analytics for clinical applications.
03

Patient & Clinician-Centred Design

I have initiated several collaborations with social scientists and patient/community organisations to co-design AI systems that are genuinely acceptable and adaptable in practice. Understanding end-users’ perspectives on these technologies is fundamental.

Co-design, Adoption & Ethics of Healthcare AI
Working with the Epilepsy Foundation, HCI researchers (Monash), and community organisations to build AI that reflects real patient and clinician needs. Papers in preparation — please contact me directly for details.
